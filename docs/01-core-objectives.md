# Prompt: Core Business Objectives

As an expert AI software engineer, your task is to ensure the **Bar Business Management Software** meets the following core business objectives.

---

## **Core Business Objectives**
- **Provide a local-first POS:** The system must function reliably for cash payment processing, even without an internet connection.
- **Deliver essential business management:** It must include modules for inventory, stock-taking, debt management, and basic accounting.
- **Ensure business continuity:** The software should be designed to prevent data loss and operational downtime during network outages.
- **Provide simple, accurate reporting:** The system must generate clear and concise reports that are essential for daily operations.

Your implementation of all features should align with these guiding principles.
