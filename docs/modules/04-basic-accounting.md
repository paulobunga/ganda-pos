# Prompt: Basic Accounting & Cash Management

As an expert AI software engineer, you will develop the **Basic Accounting & Cash Management** module. This module will provide simple tools for tracking revenue, expenses, and overall profitability.

---

### **4. Basic Accounting & Cash Management**

#### **Daily Cash Tracking:**
- **Opening Float:** Manage the opening cash float for each day.
- **Revenue Tracking:** Track sales revenue throughout the day in real-time.
- **Withdrawals & Petty Cash:** Record cash withdrawals and the usage of petty cash.
- **Reconciliation:** An end-of-day cash reconciliation tool.
- **Bank Deposits:** Prepare and record bank deposits.

#### **Simple Expense Management:**
- **Expense Recording:** A simple form to record cash expenses, including receipt capture via camera.
- **Categorization:** Allow expenses to be categorized (e.g., supplies, maintenance, utilities).
- **Petty Cash Fund:** Manage a petty cash fund separately from the cash drawer.
- **Supplier Payments:** Track cash payments made to suppliers.
- **Reporting:** Basic expense reports by category.

#### **Revenue & Profitability:**
- **Daily Totals:** Provide daily sales totals with a breakdown of cash vs. tab payments.
- **COGS:** Calculate the cost of goods sold (COGS) for products.
- **Gross Profit Margins:** Show gross profit margins by product.
- **Summaries:** Weekly and monthly revenue summaries.
- **Cash Flow:** Simple cash flow tracking (money in vs. money out).

#### **Basic Financial Reports:**
- **Daily Cash Summary:** A report showing `opening + sales - expenses = closing`.
- **Weekly Summary:** A summary of sales and expenses for the week.
- **Monthly P&L:** A simple monthly profit and loss overview.
- **Cash Flow Report:** A report showing the movement of cash.
- **Cost Analysis:** A simple cost analysis for the most popular products.

#### **User Stories to Implement:**
- **As an owner,** I want a daily cash report that shows me exactly how much money came in and went out.
- **As a manager,** I want to be able to track petty cash usage and process reimbursements easily.
- **As an accountant,** I want simple P&L reports that I can use for monthly bookkeeping.
- **As a staff member,** I want an easy way to record small expenses that I pay for in cash during my shift.
