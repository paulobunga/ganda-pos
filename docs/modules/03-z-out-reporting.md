# Prompt: Z-Out Reporting & Shift Management

As an expert AI software engineer, you will develop the **Z-Out Reporting & Shift Management** module. This module is essential for daily closing procedures and ensuring a smooth handover between shifts.

---

### **3. Z-Out Reporting & Shift Management**

#### **End-of-Shift Reporting:**
- **Shift Summary:** A complete summary of all transactions for the shift.
- **Cash Reconciliation:** A detailed breakdown of cash reconciliation, including any over/under amounts.
- **Sales Totals:** Total sales broken down by product category.
- **Tab Status:** A list of tabs that were opened, closed, and those that are still outstanding.
- **Staff Performance:** A summary of performance for the staff member who worked the shift.

#### **Z-Out Report Components:**
- **Opening Balance:** The starting cash amount in the register.
- **Sales Summary:** Total sales, broken down by payment type (cash, tab).
- **Cash Transactions:** A log of all cash sales with a running total.
- **Tab Activity:** Details on new tabs, payments received, and outstanding balances.
- **Inventory Impact:** A summary of products sold and any stock adjustments made during the shift.
- **Closing Balance:** The final cash count and the expected amount based on sales.
- **Variances:** Any discrepancies that require an explanation from the staff.

#### **Shift Handover:**
- **Report Format:** The Z-out report should be available in both a printable and digital format for manager review.
- **Notes Section:** Include a section for staff to leave notes about incidents or issues during their shift.
- **Action Items:** A list of outstanding items that require attention from the next shift.
- **Cash Drawer Status:** A confirmation of the cash drawer status for the incoming staff member.

#### **User Stories to Implement:**
- **As a closing bartender,** I want a complete Z-out report that clearly shows my performance and cash handling for the shift.
- **As an incoming manager,** I want clear handover information about any outstanding issues or tasks from the previous shift.
- **As an owner,** I want to review Z-out reports to monitor daily cash flow and identify any operational problems.
- **As an accountant,** I want shift reports that can be easily tied to the daily sales totals for bookkeeping.
