# Prompt: Point of Sale (POS) System - Cash Focused

As an expert AI software engineer, you are to build the **Point of Sale (POS) System** module for the Bar Business Management Software. This module must be optimized for a fast, touchscreen interface and focus on reliable cash transaction handling.

---

### **1. Point of Sale (POS) System - Cash Focused**

#### **Core POS Functionality:**
- **UI:** Fast, touchscreen-optimized interface.
- **Inventory:** Real-time inventory updates with each sale.
- **Tabs:** Manage multiple customer tabs with clear visual indicators.
- **Billing:** Support for split billing for group payments.
- **Discounts:** Simple discount management (percentage and fixed amounts).
- **Search:** Quick product search with categories.
- **Cash Drawer:** Integration with cash drawer, including tracking of opening/closing balances.

#### **Cash Payment Features:**
- **Cash Register Management:** Track opening balance, sales, and closing balance for each shift.
- **Change Calculation:** Automate change computation, handling large bills correctly.
- **Cash Over/Under Tracking:** Report on cash variances for each shift.
- **Denomination Counting:** Provide a tool to break down cash totals by bill and coin denominations.
- **Cash Drop Recording:** Allow staff to securely record cash removals from the drawer during a shift.

#### **User Stories to Implement:**
- **As a bartender,** I want to quickly process cash payments and give the correct change to keep service moving.
- **As a manager,** I want to track cash variances at the end of each shift to identify counting errors or potential issues.
- **As a cashier,** I want to record cash drops to maintain a safe and manageable amount of cash in the register.
- **As an owner,** I want a daily summary of all cash transactions to monitor my business's cash flow.
