# Prompt: Debt Management & Customer Tabs

As an expert AI software engineer, you will develop the **Debt Management & Customer Tabs** module. This module is designed to manage credit for trusted customers and ensure timely payments.

---

### **5. Debt Management & Customer Tabs**

#### **Customer Tab System:**
- **Tab Creation:** Allow staff to create tabs for trusted customers, with optional spending limits.
- **Adding Items:** Easily add items to a customer's tab throughout their visit.
- **Partial Payments:** Accept and track partial payments on a tab.
- **Settlement:** A clear process for full tab settlement and payment confirmation.
- **History:** Maintain a history of all transactions and payments for each tab.

#### **Credit Management:**
- **Credit Limits:** Set customer-specific credit limits and payment terms.
- **Balance Tracking:** Track outstanding balances for each customer.
- **Reminders:** A system for sending payment reminders and collection notices.
- **Trustworthiness:** A simple rating system for customer trustworthiness based on payment history.
- **Reporting:** Reports on overdue debts and customers who have exceeded their credit limits.

#### **Debt Collection Tools:**
- **Outstanding Balance Reports:** Generate reports of outstanding balances, sortable by customer.
- **Aging Analysis:** An aging report showing how long debts have been overdue (e.g., 30, 60, 90+ days).
- **Payment History:** Analyze a customer's payment patterns to identify potential issues.
- **Reminder System:** A simple, non-intrusive reminder system for overdue accounts.
- **Write-Off:** A procedure for writing off uncollectable debts.

#### **Tab Monitoring:**
- **Real-Time Balances:** Display real-time tab balances during service.
- **Daily Summaries:** A summary of all outstanding debt at the end of each day.
- **Spending Analysis:** Analyze customer spending patterns on their tabs.
- **Payment Trends:** Track trends in tab payments over time.
- **Bad Debt Prevention:** Tools to help identify and prevent bad debt before it accumulates.

#### **User Stories to Implement:**
- **As a bartender,** I want to quickly and easily add items to a regular customer's tab.
- **As a manager,** I want a report that shows me all outstanding customer debts and who is overdue.
- **As an owner,** I want to be able to identify customers with payment issues early on.
- **As a staff member,** I want a warning when a customer is approaching their credit limit.
