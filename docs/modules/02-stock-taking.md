# Prompt: Stock-Taking System

As an expert AI software engineer, you will develop the **Stock-Taking System** module. This module is critical for maintaining accurate inventory and identifying discrepancies.

---

### **2. Stock-Taking System**

#### **Physical Stock Counting:**
- **Interface:** A mobile-friendly interface for counting stock directly from the stockroom.
- **Product Lookup:** Allow staff to find products by name or by scanning a barcode.
- **Count Entry:** Flexible count entry with support for different units (e.g., bottles, cases, liters).
- **Partial Counts:** The ability to save a partial count and resume it later.
- **Photo Capture:** Allow staff to capture photos of damaged or questionable items directly within the app.

#### **Stock Variance & Reporting:**
- **Comparison:** Automatically compare physical counts against the system's inventory records.
- **Variance Highlighting:** Clearly highlight significant variances that require investigation.
- **Stock Adjustments:** An approval workflow for making automatic stock adjustments based on count results.
- **Shrinkage Reporting:** Generate reports on shrinkage, categorized by product type.
- **Historical Tracking:** Track variance history to identify recurring patterns or problem areas.

#### **Stock-Taking Workflows:**
- **Full Stock Take:** Support for complete inventory counts (typically weekly or monthly).
- **Spot Checks:** Enable random product verification to ensure ongoing accuracy.
- **Category Counts:** Allow staff to focus on specific product categories (e.g., beer, wine).
- **Damage Assessment:** A simple workflow for recording breakage and waste as it happens.
- **Expiry Management:** Track products that are approaching their expiration dates.

#### **User Stories to Implement:**
- **As a manager,** I want to conduct weekly stock takes to ensure my inventory records are accurate.
- **As a bartender,** I want a quick way to report a broken bottle during my shift.
- **As an owner,** I want variance reports that help me identify potential theft or waste issues.
- **As a staff member,** I want a simple tool to count stock that doesn't require complex procedures or calculations.
