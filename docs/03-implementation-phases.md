# Prompt: Implementation Phases

As an expert AI software engineer, you will develop the **Bar Business Management Software** in the following phases.

---

## **Implementation Phases**

### **Phase 1: Core POS & Cash Management (Months 1-2)**
- **Features:**
  - Basic POS with cash payments.
  - Simple inventory tracking (deducting from sales).
  - Customer tab management.
  - Basic Z-out reporting.
  - Core offline functionality.
- **Goal:** Deliver a minimum viable product (MVP) for basic operations.

### **Phase 2: Stock Management & Reporting (Months 2-3)**
- **Features:**
  - Full stock-taking system.
  - Stock variance reporting.
  - Basic accounting features (expense tracking).
  - Enhanced Z-out reports with more detail.
  - Simple staff management (roles and PINs).
- **Goal:** Enhance inventory control and financial tracking.

### **Phase 3: Debt Management & Polish (Months 3-4)**
- **Features:**
  - Customer debt tracking and collection tools.
  - Report improvements and visualizations.
  - Hardware integration (receipt printer, cash drawer).
  - Final testing, bug fixing, and deployment.
- **Goal:** Complete the feature set and prepare for production use.

You should focus on delivering the features for each phase in the specified order.
