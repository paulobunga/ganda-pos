# Prompt 2: Inventory & Stock-Taking Module

Create the **Inventory and Stock-Taking module**.

## Requirements:
- Inventory CRUD with:
  - Product categories, stock levels, costs, taxes, and expiry dates.
- Stock-taking workflow:
  - Record physical count.
  - Show expected vs. actual.
  - Log discrepancies with reasons and timestamps.
- Alerts for low stock and near-expiry products.
- Integration with POS to adjust stock in real-time.
- Offline-first with sync to server when online.

## Deliverables:
- Inventory management UI and logic.
- Stock-taking page.
- Reports for discrepancies and low-stock alerts.
- Unit tests for stock adjustments and reconciliation.
