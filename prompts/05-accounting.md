# Prompt 5: Sales & Accounting Module

Develop the **Sales and Accounting module**.

## Requirements:
- Track all sales (POS, tabs, promotions).
- Record cash in/out (petty cash, supplier payments, expenses).
- Auto-generate basic Profit & Loss summaries.
- Integrate with reporting module for financial statements.

## Deliverables:
- Sales summary dashboard.
- Cash log UI.
- P&L calculation logic.
- Unit tests for sales and expense records.
