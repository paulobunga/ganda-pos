# Prompt 4: Purchase & Supplier Management

Create the **Purchase and Supplier Management module**.

## Requirements:
- CRUD for suppliers.
- Purchase order creation:
  - Items, cost, tax, supplier details.
- Mark orders as received:
  - Update stock automatically.
- Track outstanding supplier payments.
- Offline storage and sync.

## Deliverables:
- Supplier UI and logic.
- Purchase order creation and receiving workflows.
- Integration tests for stock updates after receiving.
