# Prompt 3: Customer & Tab Management

Build the **Customer and Tab management module**.

## Requirements:
- Create and manage customer profiles with name, phone, and "trusted" status.
- Tab system for debts:
  - Add items to tabs.
  - Partial or full settlement.
  - Debt history logs.
- Alerts for existing debts when creating new ones.
- Offline storage with sync queue.
- Reporting of outstanding balances.

## Deliverables:
- Customer profile UI.
- Tab management UI.
- IndexedDB integration.
- Unit tests for tab creation, updates, and settlements.
